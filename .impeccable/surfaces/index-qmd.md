# Home (index.qmd)

Scope: the site's front door. Mode: Experience. Audiences equal: hiring visitors (credible depth in a minute) and learners (find and return to notes). The world chosen here replaces the site-wide theme.

Constraints: no invented stats, logos, employers or testimonials; no flashy motion; not a CV page; not plain. Owner surname, photo, contact links and CV pending; "Minh" awaits confirmation. User decisions (2026-11-16): About content opens Home (no separate About page); no "Recently published" table; notes and sources live inside lecture/topic pages, not at tab level; navbar tabs open dropdown menus; all four study-map lanes stay, empty ones as one quiet "Planned" line.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Home is a page from a research article: the person, then Figure 1, a live study map read like a plotted figure, then Figure 2, the Lab. It refuses the hero-plus-equal-cards portfolio template.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white page (#fdfdfc), ink #15171c. Four domain inks used semantically everywhere: ML vermilion, Math cobalt, Data green, System violet; Lab in ink. Numbered figures with italic captions, hairline rules. Status by line form (solid published, dotted in progress, dashed planned), never hue. Depth only from rules and overlap; no shadows, no gradients.

STORY: The visitor learns who the owner is and is handed named entry points (latest lecture, active course, Lab), sees four domains at a glance with real and planned work, and clicks into a lecture, a course or the Lab.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, about 2/3 width: the owner's name as the serif display, role beneath in grotesk, a short bio. Right column under a 2px ink rule: a generated "Start here" list (latest lecture, courses with written-up lectures, the Lab), later joined by contact details. Below: Figure 1 study map, each lane note naming its latest lecture as a link; then Figure 2, Lab plates.

FORM: Distill-style figure-led research article; position 5 of 7 on the grounded list; seed:779446935dee947a. Raises: emission rail (status as line form), seven-segment (fixed slots, absence drawn), papercut (no shadows), matchbook (each Lab item one complete captioned plate).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
